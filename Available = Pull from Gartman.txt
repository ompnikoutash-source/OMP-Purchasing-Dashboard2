Available = Pull from Gartman
On PO = Pull from Gartman
Backorder (Qty) = Pull from Gartman
Minimum Order Quantity = Leave as zero for now
Days per Month Conversion = 30.4 use this below
Lead Time (Days) = either from Lead Time Excel or Gartman, prioritize Excel
Average Monthly Demand = Take average of the historical demand
Daily Mean of Demand = Average Monthly Demand / 30.4
SD of Demand (Monthly) = std of the historical monthly demand
Daily Standard Deviation = SD of Demand (Monthly) / sqrt(30.4)
Lead Time Mean Demand = Lead Time * Daily Mean of Demand
Standard Deviation of Demand over LT = Daily Standard Deviation * sqrt(lead time in days)
Service Level = .95
Z-Score of Service Level = Z score of .95
Safety Stock (SS) = Z-Score of Service Level * STD of Demand over Lead Time
Reorder Point (ROP) = Lead Time Mean Demand + Safety Stock
